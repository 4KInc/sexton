# What the register shows

- The register lists this object as COSMOS 2440 [register: name], NORAD catalogue number 33108 [register: norad].
- The catalogue's current status is "retired payload" [register: status]. The status comes from SATCAT [register: statusSource].
- The owner code is CIS [register: owner].
- The launch date is 2008-06-27 [register: launchDate].
- The whole years from the launch date to the snapshot date are 18 [register: verdict.yearsSinceLaunch]. This is not an age at the end of operation.

GEO is the circular orbit about 35,786 km above the equator, where a satellite stays over one spot [register: config.geoAltitudeKm]. Perigee is the lowest point of an orbit. In this register it is given as the distance above GEO, so a negative value is below GEO. Eccentricity is how far an orbit is from a perfect circle. 0 is a circle [docs/methodology.md: definition of eccentricity].

- The perigee is 24.7 km below GEO, which is −24.7 km above GEO [register: pKm].
- The apogee, the highest point of the orbit, is 32.2 km above GEO [register: qKm].
- The eccentricity is 0.0006746 [register: e].
- The inclination is 10.88° [register: inclinationDeg].

Sexton's verdict is "unquiet": the perigee is at or below the top of the protected zone, so the orbit reaches the protected zone [register: verdict.verdict]. The protected zone is GEO ± 200 km [register: config.protectedZoneKm]. The verdict is not borderline [register: verdict.borderline]. A verdict is borderline when the perigee is within 25 km of a band edge [register: config.uncertaintyKm]. The perigee is 224.7 km below the 200 km zone edge [register: verdict.margins.zone].

The IADC guidelines are voluntary [IADC-02-01 Revision 2: https://orbitaldebris.jsc.nasa.gov/library/IADC-Space-Debris-Guidelines-Revision-2.pdf]. The FCC minimum-perigee rule (47 CFR § 25.283) applies only to satellites the FCC authorizes, launched on or after 18 March 2002 [47 CFR § 25.283: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/25.283]. This satellite was launched on 2008-06-27 [register: launchDate], after that date. The register does not say whether the FCC authorized it.

# Assumptions and data age

The verdict assumes a C_R·A/m between 0.012 and 0.045 m²/kg [register: config.iadc.crAmMin] [register: config.iadc.crAmMax]. These are Sexton's assumptions. They are not measured for this satellite. The verdict also allows a mean-element uncertainty of ±25 km [register: config.uncertaintyKm].

The orbit comes from GP mean elements [register: orbitSource.kind]. The GP epoch is 2026-11-22T04:43:37.675776 [register: orbitSource.epoch]. The snapshot is 2026-11-22 [register: snapshot.set].
