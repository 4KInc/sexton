# What the sources say

The IADC guidelines are voluntary. The IADC text says organisations are "encouraged" to use them [IADC-02-01 Revision 2: https://orbitaldebris.jsc.nasa.gov/library/IADC-Space-Debris-Guidelines-Revision-2.pdf].

The FCC minimum-perigee rule (47 CFR § 25.283) applies only to satellites the FCC authorizes. Paragraph (d) excludes space stations launched before March 18, 2002 [47 CFR § 25.283: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/25.283]. This satellite's launch date is on or after that date [register: launchDate] [register: config.fccRuleDate]. The register cannot tell whether the FCC authorized this satellite.
