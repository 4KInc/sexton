- The FCC minimum-perigee rule, 47 CFR § 25.283, applies only to satellites the FCC authorizes, launched on or after 18 March 2002. [47 CFR § 25.283: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/25.283]
- The launch date in the register meets the launch-date test of that rule. [register: launchDate] [register: config.fccRuleDate]
