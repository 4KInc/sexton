# What the sources say

- The IADC Space Debris Mitigation Guidelines are voluntary. Section 2 says organisations are "encouraged" to use them [IADC-02-01 Revision 2: https://orbitaldebris.jsc.nasa.gov/library/IADC-Space-Debris-Guidelines-Revision-2.pdf].
- The FCC minimum-perigee rule (47 CFR § 25.283) applies only to satellites the FCC authorizes. Paragraph (d) excludes space stations launched before March 18, 2002 [47 CFR § 25.283: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/25.283].
- The register holds a launch date of 2011-09-18, which is on or after 2002-03-18 [register: launchDate] [register: config.fccRuleDate]. The register cannot tell whether the FCC authorized this satellite, so it makes no statement about whether that rule applies.
