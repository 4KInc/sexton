# Assumptions and data age

- The IADC formula asks for a perigee at least 235 km plus 1000 times C_R·A/m above GEO. [register: config.iadc.baseKm] [register: config.iadc.factorKm]
- Sexton assumes C_R·A/m between 0.012 and 0.045 m²/kg. [register: config.iadc.crAmMin] [register: config.iadc.crAmMax]
- Sexton assumes a mean-element uncertainty of ±25 km. [register: config.uncertaintyKm]
- The orbit comes from GP mean elements with epoch 2026-11-22T10:26:44.863008. [register: orbitSource.epoch]
- The data set is the snapshot of 2026-11-22. [register: snapshot.set]

The IADC guidelines are voluntary. [IADC-02-01 Revision 2: https://orbitaldebris.jsc.nasa.gov/library/IADC-Space-Debris-Guidelines-Revision-2.pdf]

The FCC minimum-perigee rule (47 CFR § 25.283) applies only to satellites the FCC authorizes. Its paragraph (d) excludes space stations launched before March 18, 2002. [47 CFR § 25.283: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/25.283]

This satellite was launched on or after the rule date of 2002-03-18. The register does not say whether the FCC authorized it. [register: launchDate] [register: config.fccRuleDate]
